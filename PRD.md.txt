# Product Requirements Document: High-Converting Google Sheet Habit Tracker Landing Page

## Objective
Build a standalone, high-converting single-product landing page designed to outperform trackkar.store for selling a Google Sheet Habit Tracker via Meta Ads.

## Target Audience & Angles
- Cold Meta Ads traffic seeking simple, low-friction habit tracking.
- Positioning: Fast, zero-subscription, automated progress tracking directly inside Google Sheets.

## Section Breakdown (Conversion Stack)
1. Announcement Bar: High-contrast header highlighting a limited-time discount.
2. Hero Section: Headline, subheadline, 3 key feature bullets, high-res dashboard preview image, and a primary CTA button linking to checkout.
3. Problem/Solution Grid: Comparing cluttered habit apps vs. your clean Google Sheet tracker.
4. Core Features Showcase: Card layout detailing Visual Progress Bars, Streak Counters, Monthly Analytics, and Mobile View.
5. 3-Step Setup Process: Buy -> Click 'Make a Copy' -> Start Tracking.
6. Social Proof / Testimonials: 3-4 customer review cards with 5-star ratings.
7. FAQ Section: Interactive accordion addressing top buying objections.
8. Sticky Mobile CTA: Persistent bar fixed to the bottom on small screens with price and a direct buy button.

## Design & Styling
- Framework: HTML + Tailwind CSS (via CDN or Vite build).
- Color Palette: Clean modern dark/light contrast with a high-visibility CTA accent color (e.g., Emerald Green or Bright Indigo).
- Typography: Clean sans-serif fonts (Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans).

## Integrations
- Meta Pixel in `<head>` (tracking PageView and InitiateCheckout).
- CTA buttons configured to trigger payment checkout.
